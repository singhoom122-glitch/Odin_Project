Chilli Chicken

Chilli Chicken is a popular Indo-Chinese dish made with crispy chicken pieces tossed in a spicy, tangy, and flavorful sauce. It is usually prepared with green chillies, capsicum, onion, garlic, and soy sauce.


Ingridents

  • 250g chicken, boneless
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 capsicum, chopped
  • 3–4 green chillies
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp chilli sauce
  • 1 tbsp tomato ketchup
  • 1 tbsp tomato ketchup
  • 1 tbsp tomato ketchup
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour (maida)
  • 1 egg
  • Salt – as needed
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • Oil – for frying
  • ½ cup water

Making process .....

  1. Marinate chicken: Mix chicken with egg, cornflour, maida, salt, and black pepper.
  2. Fry chicken: Heat oil and fry the chicken pieces until golden and crispy. Prepare vegetables: Heat a little oil and sauté onion, capsicum, green chillies, and ginger-garlic paste.
  3. Add sauces: Add soy sauce, chilli sauce, and tomato ketchup. Mix well. Add water: Pour in a little water and cook for 1–2 minutes.
  4. Add chicken: Put the fried chicken into the sauce and toss well. Cook: Cook for another 2–3 minutes until the sauce coats the chicken.
  5. Serve: Garnish with spring onions and serve hot with fried rice or noodles. 🌶️🍗
