Chicken Butter Masala

Chicken butter masala is a rich, creamy, and mildly spicy Indian curry made with tender marinated chicken in a smooth tomato and butter sauce.


    Ingrident

  • Chicken: 500g boneless chicken pieces.
  • Marinade: 1 tsp chili powder, a pinch of turmeric, 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste, and salt.
  • Gravy Base: 3-4 chopped tomatoes, 2-3 sliced onions, 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste, and 10-12 cashew nuts.
  • Spices & Flavorings: 1 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp coriander powder, Kashmiri chili powder (to taste), and 2 tsp dried fenugreek leaves (kasuri methi)
  • Dairy & Fats: 3-5 tbsp butter, 2-3 tbsp fresh cream, and a splash of milk or water.

    Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Marinate the chicken: Mix the chicken pieces with chili powder, turmeric, ginger-garlic paste, and salt. Let it rest for 15 minutes.
  2. Pan-fry the chicken: Heat a little oil and butter in a pan and sear the chicken pieces until lightly golden. Set them aside.
  3. Make the base: In the same pan, sauté the onions, tomatoes, cashew nuts, and ginger-garlic paste with a little water until soft.
  4. Blend to a smooth paste: Allow the mixture to cool slightly, then blend it into a completely smooth puree.
  5. Simmer the gravy: Melt more butter in the pan, add the pureed masala along with dry spices (garam masala, coriander powder, chili powder), and cook until oil separates.
  6. Combine and finish: Add the cooked chicken and water if needed, simmering for 5 to 10 minutes. Stir in fresh cream and crushed kasuri methi before serving.
